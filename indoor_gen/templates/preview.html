        Rooms
          Pick a tool in the top bar (select, polygon, rectangle, move / rotate / scale, delete, undo; switch to the floor map with the Rooms / Floor map switch). With Select, Shift+click several rooms to merge them, drag a vertex to move it, click an edge to add a vertex, drag an edge's "○" to move the whole edge in parallel. Vertices snap to nearby vertices, walls and right angles (hold Alt to turn this off). Editing is done in the top-down view.
          Move the floor map
          Room edits are not saved. Before saving the floor map, go back to "Rooms" and press "Save & apply".
          Move the shown floor map: rotate it with the slider, move and scale it by dragging on the map (the centre of the building outline is the reference). Drag the image to move it, drag the corner and edge squares to scale (the opposite side stays fixed); Option+wheel also scales around the pointer (the wheel alone zooms the map). Only the image moves while you adjust. "Save this floor only" moves the shown floor's map and rooms; "Apply to all floors" moves every floor by the same amount.
